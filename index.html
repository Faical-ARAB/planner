Daily targets

Daily goal
Healthy weight gain
2,900kcal / day
117–139 g protein
  • Aim for a gentle +0.25–0.5 kg/week
  • Hit calories through snacks — your job to prep
  • Add olive oil, nuts, dates, full-fat dairy & eggs
  • Strength training 3×/week builds muscle, not just fat
Daily goal
Breastfeeding
2,300–2,500kcal / day
75–90 g protein
  • Eat to appetite — nursing burns ~450–500 extra kcal
  • Keep water + a prepped snack where you feed (~2.5–3 L fluids)
  • Iron, calcium & omega-3 at most meals
  • No-cook breakfasts & dinners by design
The system
Low effort by design
  • One real cook a day — lunch; make a little extra
  • Breakfast & dinner are no-cook or quick sandwiches
  • The non-cook preps snacks in weekend batches
  • Mostly modern halal meals; Friday is the weekend classic
  • Saturday = relaxed breakfast + leftover dinner

Snacks to batch-prep

High-calorie · prep once
For weight gain
  • Energy balls: dates, oats, peanut butter, nuts — roll a dozen
  • Boiled eggs by the half-dozen, kept in the fridge
  • Trail mix: nuts + dried figs/raisins in small bags
  • Overnight-oats jars (milk, oats, dates, peanut butter)
  • Cheese + cracker + fruit packs
One-handed · while feeding
For breastfeeding
  • Dates + almonds within reach of where you feed
  • Yogurt or rayeb pots — calcium + hydration
  • A pre-made sandwich or wrap in the fridge
  • A bottle of water at every feeding spot
  • Warm milk with honey for night feeds

Make it sustainable

Cook once, eat twice

Lunch is the only real cook. Make a little extra and the next dinner is sorted with zero work.

The non-cook owns snacks & shopping

Whoever doesn’t cook can still own the no-cook prep: a weekend snack session and the weekly shop take real load off the main cook.

Share the cooking days

With a one-month-old, take the easy lunches — pasta, burgers and oven trays are hard to get wrong.

Stock shortcuts

Canned tuna & chickpeas, frozen veg, eggs, bread and yogurt carry the no-cook meals on the hardest days.

Repetition is fine

Eating the same good lunch a few days running is normal right now. Variety can wait.
